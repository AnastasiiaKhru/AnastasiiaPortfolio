Reserved Monthly Support Hours

Website Care Plans
You Choose How Hours Are Used

Each plan reserves a set number of professional support hours every month. You decide what needs attention, submit a request, and those hours are applied only to the work you approve — within your plan’s eligible service scope.

01

You purchase hours

Your plan locks in a monthly block of support time (4, 8, or 12 hours). You are buying capacity — not an automatic checklist of tasks.

02

You choose the work

When something needs updating, fixing, or improving, you tell us what you need. Eligible services are listed under each plan — you pick from that scope.

03

We complete the request

We estimate time, confirm the request, complete the work, and deduct hours from your monthly balance. Nothing happens until you request it.

Important

The services listed under each plan are the types of work your hours may be used for — they are not delivered automatically. You select what to prioritize. Hours are applied only after you submit a request and we confirm the work. Requests outside your plan’s eligible scope are quoted separately and require your written approval before any work begins.
